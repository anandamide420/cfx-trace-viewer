Calculation and interpretation

Imported LIDAR and SYBR standard curves

Six tenfold gBlock dilutions per assay, spanning five log intervals. Points and fitted coefficients are imported from the prior publication dataset, rather than generated from the EN9581 plate.

Original calibration amplification traces

These are the original exported amplification RFUs. Imported standard-curve Cqs and fitted coefficients remain fixed; EN9581’s baseline and threshold controls do not recompute them.

Primer-aligned amplicon lengths

Exact matches to the bundled reference gBlocks, counted from the first forward-primer base through the last reverse-primer base. Original oligo names and instrument labels are retained for traceability.

Column 1 uses Pfizer_Spike_40bp (46 bp aligned) and Ori_48bp (48 bp aligned). The Ori choice follows the operator’s initial identification; alignment verifies the sequence span, not which oligo was pipetted. The alternate Ori_45bp oligo would produce 45 bp.

Preparation and provenance

Vials supplied for EN9581 testing

Amplicon array

160 µl water + 20 µl 1% Triton X-100 + 20 µl vaccine produced a 1:10 vaccine dilution and 0.1% Triton. Diluting 100 µl of this mixture into 900 µl water produced 1:100 vaccine and 0.01% Triton. A 2 µl aliquot represents 0.02 µl original vaccine; in the instrument-recorded 20 µl reaction, Triton is 0.001%.

gBlocks: target-specific stock defaults of 1.47375 ng/µl Spike and 1.01 ng/µl Ori (four-value means below), diluted 1:10,000, 2 µl per reaction. The remade controls in the 14 October run contain 1:10,000 of each target; the original 13 October row C input remains unconfirmed. The 400 bp mass convention is retained from the operator-confirmed calibration; the bundled reference sequences are 401 nt. Changing the mass convention to 401 bp reduces inferred copy counts by approximately 0.25%.

Row assignments, dilution details, 2 µl template inputs and the new run’s pooled reverse-primer dilution were supplied by Kevin McKernan; the remade controls and pooled reverse-primer dilution were confirmed for the 14 October run. Instrument Sample labels are preserved in the original data. The biological assay map takes precedence over nominal length names for fitting.

gBlock stock concentration measurements

Calculator defaults use the equal-weight arithmetic mean of the four supplied values for each target: Spike (1 + 0.535 + 1.36 + 3.0) / 4 = 1.47375 ng/µl; Ori (1 + 0.340 + 0.700 + 2.0) / 4 = 1.01 ng/µl. These editable defaults apply to both EN9581 runs. The IDT value was supplied as the manufacturer-reported concentration; the other values were read from the operator’s measurement screenshots. Electrophoresis uses the main-peak concentration and excludes markers and minor higher-size peaks.

This is an operator-selected arithmetic average across methods, not four replicate measurements or an uncertainty-weighted estimate. No additional premeasurement dilution correction is applied. The average changes the independently supplied stock input; vaccine Cqs are not used to adjust it. Historical standard-curve points and efficiencies retain their original reported calibration basis.

The proposed 1:100,000 gBlock dilution for Run 1 remains unconfirmed. Its dilution default remains 1:10,000 and can be edited independently of the stock concentrations.

Historical efficiency calibration

Original LIDAR and SYBR dilution points, fitted coefficients and historical traces are included in Standard curves. This viewer contains two EN9581 plates and three historical calibration runs. The selected run determines which measured efficiencies are used. Missing or rejected efficiencies are never replaced silently. Calibration transfer to another run, reaction mixture or primer concentration remains an assumption.
